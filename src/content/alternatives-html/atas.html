What ATAS Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: the 400+ footprint chart types and the 70+ volume tools. ATAS's variety of footprint visualizations (delta, POC clusters, imbalance, ladder profile, etc) is genuinely deeper than any competitor. Different market conditions warrant different footprint views — bid/ask imbalance during breakouts, delta during trend continuation, POC clusters at S/R. ATAS lets you switch between them fluidly.

Volume tools are similarly comprehensive. Volume Profile, VWAP variants, Market Delta, cumulative delta, absorption studies — ATAS packages the full professional volume-analysis toolkit into one platform. Bookmap focuses on DOM visualization; ATAS covers everything volume-related.
