When IG Markets Isn't the Right Fit

IG Markets is the world's largest CFD and spread-betting provider with 17,000+ markets, ProRealTime charting, and 50 years of trading heritage. Real institutional pedigree. Three limits push traders elsewhere: CFD/spread-betting focused (US clients get a limited forex-only version), higher-tier features locked behind account minimums or high volume, and inactivity fees if you don't trade regularly.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you're US-based and want serious forex + multi-asset: Forex.com or Interactive Brokers. If you want tighter ECN spreads (non-US): IC Markets or FxPro. If you want CFDs without inactivity fees: OANDA or CMC Markets. If you want social/copy trading with CFDs: eToro.

What IG Markets Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: the 17,000+ market coverage and ProRealTime charting. No CFD peer offers as broad a market catalog as IG — index CFDs, forex, commodities, individual stocks across global markets, bonds, ETFs, crypto CFDs. If you want a single account to trade nearly anything as a CFD, IG's breadth is unmatched.

ProRealTime charting (available via IG for eligible clients) is one of the strongest professional charting platforms in retail trading. For traders who value serious technical analysis, this is a differentiator vs stripped-down broker platforms.
