Why Traders Outgrow Public.com

Public.com is a commission-free social investing platform combining stocks, ETFs, options, crypto, and bonds with a built-in investor community and Alpha AI-powered insights. Its social + bonds combination is genuinely novel for a US retail broker. But it hits ceilings quickly for active traders: no desktop or web charting tools for technical analysis, no paper trading or backtesting, and not suited for advanced order types or futures trading.

Where to go depends on what's missing. If you need real charts: Fidelity's Active Trader Pro, thinkorswim, or TradingView + broker. If you want options depth: tastytrade or thinkorswim. If you need professional-grade tools: Interactive Brokers. If you value social/community alongside serious tools: Webull.

What Public.com Does Genuinely Well

Two features stand out: the community layer and bond access alongside stocks. Few US retail brokers offer both community/sentiment features AND retail-friendly bond investing in one account. Alpha AI provides plain-language stock analysis that's more useful than a spreadsheet for many investors.

Commission-free options at $0/contract is competitive — cheaper than most brokers, though tastytrade's $1 to open + $0 to close structure wins for active options traders. Public.com's fractional shares work down to $1, which lowers the barrier for regular investing habits.

The Bottom Line

Public.com is the social investing + bonds broker with Alpha AI. If those specific features are why you signed up, no direct alternative combines all three in one platform. If you're leaving for charting/analysis depth, Fidelity or Webull. For options depth, tastytrade or thinkorswim. For serious institutional tools, Interactive Brokers.
