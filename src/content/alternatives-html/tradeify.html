Why Traders Look Beyond Tradeify

Tradeify is a futures-only prop firm offering Growth, Select, and Lightning plans with EOD (end-of-day) drawdown and simplified evaluation mechanics. The EOD drawdown is genuinely trader-friendly compared to intraday-drawdown firms. Three drivers push traders elsewhere: futures-only (no forex or stocks), account sizes cap at ~$150K, and US-centric — limited support outside NA hours.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want futures + more account sizes: Apex or Topstep. If you want forex + multi-asset prop: FTMO or FundedNext. If you want 1-step evaluation: SabioTrade. If you want backed by regulated broker: ThinkCapital.

What Tradeify Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: the EOD drawdown and the Lightning plan structure. EOD drawdown means intraday drawdown doesn't count against your account — only end-of-day balance matters. That's a significant edge for scalpers and momentum traders who sometimes take wider drawdowns intraday but close positive. Most prop firms use trailing intraday drawdown, which kills accounts on temporary drawdowns.

Lightning plan (1-step, faster payout) suits swing traders who don't want to grind through 2-phase evaluations. Combined with the EOD rule, Tradeify has a genuinely differentiated ruleset — not just another FTMO clone.
