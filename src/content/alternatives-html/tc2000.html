Where TC2000 Hits Its Ceiling

TC2000 is a 30+ year US-equity scanner and charting platform from Worden Brothers, best known for its EasyScan plain-English screening language and integrated brokerage. It's a legitimately strong tool for US stock scanners who don't want to write code. But traders leave it for three main reasons: no futures / forex / crypto, no order-flow tools (no footprint, no DOM, no volume profile), and no strategy backtesting or automation. The best alternative depends on which of those you need.

If you need multi-asset coverage, the answer isn't a peer scanner — it's TradingView (multi-asset, community indicators) or NinjaTrader (futures-first). If you need order flow, look at Bookmap or Sierra Chart. If you need backtesting and automation, TradingView Pine Script or TradeStation's EasyLanguage.

What TC2000 Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things are hard to replace: EasyScan plain-English screening and real-time scan speed on the US stock universe. EasyScan means non-technical traders can build sophisticated scans without writing code — this is genuinely better than TradingView's community screeners or Finviz's dropdown filters when you want unusual condition combinations. The scan engine speed is best-in-class for the price point.

Integrated brokerage with competitive commissions also matters — it means scan-to-trade doesn't require exporting tickers to a separate broker platform. TradeStation and thinkorswim have this same benefit; TradingView requires broker connections that don't always work as cleanly.

The Bottom Line

TC2000 remains the best plain-English US-stock scanner + charting + broker platform. If you never need futures, order flow, backtesting, or non-US markets, there isn't a stronger direct alternative — you'd be trading down. If you need any of those, the alternative depends on which one: TradingView for multi-asset, NinjaTrader for futures, TradeStation for backtesting, thinkorswim for options analytics. For free scanning, start with Finviz before paying anything.
