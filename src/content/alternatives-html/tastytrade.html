Why Someone Looks Beyond Tastytrade

Tastytrade — built by thinkorswim's original founders (Tom Sosnoff, Tony Battista, Scott Sheridan) — is the options-first broker with the cheapest options commissions in the US market: $1 to open, $0 to close, capped at $10/leg. Stock and ETF commissions are $0. tastylive (the accompanying content network) is the largest options education library in retail. It's genuinely great at options. Where it hits ceilings: basic charting compared to TradingView or thinkorswim, no advanced scripting or custom indicators, and no order-flow tools (no footprint, volume profile, DOM).

If your gap is chart depth: TradingView + broker integration or thinkorswim. If you need order flow: Bookmap or Sierra Chart. If you want multi-asset execution (futures, forex, global): Interactive Brokers.

What Tastytrade Does Better Than Everyone

Two things: options commissions and tastylive content. The commission structure ($1 to open, $0 to close, capped at $10/leg for large orders) is measurably cheaper than every competitor for active options traders. On a 10-leg iron condor, tastytrade costs $10 to open vs $15 at Schwab/Fidelity — the savings compound at volume.

tastylive is the biggest options-education network in retail. Free daily shows on strategy, market analysis, and trade breakdowns. Whether you use tastytrade or another broker, tastylive is worth watching. The two together (broker + education) make tastytrade the reference options-only stack.

Feature Trade-offs vs Alternatives

  • Tastytrade: $1 open / $0 close options, $0 stocks, basic charts, no scripting, no order flow, no futures on retail account (but has futures for eligible traders).
  • thinkorswim: $0.65/contract options, $0 stocks, professional charts, ThinkScript, options analytics, futures.
  • TradingView + broker: Best charts, Pine Script, plus your broker's execution. Options depend on broker.
  • Interactive Brokers: $0.65/contract, institutional-tier pricing on volume. Multi-asset global.
  • Charles Schwab: $0.65/contract, includes thinkorswim. Full wealth management.
  • Robinhood: $0/contract options but limited analytics and PFOF concerns.

The Bottom Line

Tastytrade is the cheapest US options broker for active traders. If you trade options daily and want the lowest commissions with a functional platform + education layer, no alternative beats it on pure cost. If your gap is charting, add TradingView alongside.
