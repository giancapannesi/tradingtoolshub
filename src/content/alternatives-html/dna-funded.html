What DNA Funded Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: the broker-backed infrastructure and the program-type menu breadth. Being backed by an actual broker (rather than existing purely as an evaluation shop) means execution quality, order routing, and payout security benefit from broker-grade infrastructure. Pure evaluation firms operate in a gray zone that regulators are increasingly scrutinizing; broker-backed firms sit on more stable ground.

The 4-program choice (1-Phase, 2-Phase, Rapid, Instant Funding) means traders can pick a challenge matching their trading style and cash constraints. Aggressive traders take Rapid or Instant. Conservative traders take 2-Phase for lower fees.
