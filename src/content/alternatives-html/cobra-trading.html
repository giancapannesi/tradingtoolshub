Why Traders Look Beyond Cobra Trading

Cobra Trading is a direct-market-access broker for active US equity day traders — DAS Trader Pro integration, competitive short locates, tight commissions on high volume, and no PDT rule for offshore accounts. The niche is real. Three drivers push traders elsewhere: minimum funding is high ($30K on some plans), options coverage is thin compared to full-service brokers, and platform fees add up for casual traders.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want similar DMA + short locates: Lightspeed or SpeedTrader. If you want lowest commissions period: Interactive Brokers. If you want professional options tools: thinkorswim. If you want zero-commission stocks + options: Webull or tastytrade.

What Cobra Trading Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: deep short-locate inventory for hard-to-borrow shorts and personal white-glove service. Cobra actively hunts locates on low-float momentum names that other brokers can't source or charge extortionate fees for. For traders who short small-cap runners, that access is genuinely differentiated. Personal risk-desk relationships mean edge cases (locate requests, borrow renegotiations, platform issues) get human attention faster than at Interactive Brokers or Webull.

DAS Trader Pro integration is professional-grade. Hot keys, montages, level 2 depth, and order-routing options mirror institutional trading desks. For traders who value execution speed over UI polish, DAS is the standard.
