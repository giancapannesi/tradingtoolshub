When Finviz Isn't Enough

Finviz is the best free stock screener in retail — 60+ filters across fundamentals, technicals, and descriptive criteria, iconic S&P 500 heat maps, insider trading data, and news aggregation. Millions use it daily without ever paying. Three limits push traders elsewhere: US stocks only (no international coverage), Elite tier at $39.50/mo doesn't add pro-charting depth, and no options screening or options-flow data.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want multi-asset scanning (futures/forex/crypto): TradingView. If you want plain-English screening + broker execution: TC2000. If you want AI-driven scanning: Trade Ideas. If you want deep fundamentals + portfolio analysis: Stock Rover. If you want options screening: Unusual Whales or OptionStrat.

What Finviz Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: free screener speed and breadth and the iconic heat maps. The Finviz screener processes 60+ filters across thousands of US stocks in fractions of a second — genuinely best-in-class for the free tier. Elite adds real-time data + intraday charts + premarket/after-hours scanning, which is worth paying for if you scan actively during market hours.

Heat maps are visually best-in-class. The S&P 500 heat map is embedded on financial news sites for a reason — it's the fastest visual summary of the market at any given moment. No competitor matches Finviz's heat map quality.
