When TradeZero Isn't the Right Fit

TradeZero is a commission-free broker with a standout short-locate system — for active traders who need to short small-cap or hard-to-borrow stocks, this is genuinely differentiated. Free and paid tiers cater to different volume levels. Three limits push traders elsewhere: US clients limited to TradeZero America product (features vary from International), margin rates higher than Interactive Brokers, and lighter research + charting tools vs full-service brokers.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want lowest margin + institutional pricing: Interactive Brokers. If you want direct-access day trading: Lightspeed or SpeedTrader. If you want options-first + cheap options: tastytrade or thinkorswim. If you want full-service $0 commissions: Fidelity or Schwab.

What TradeZero Does Better Than Alternatives

The short-locate system is TradeZero's genuine differentiator. Many brokers restrict short-selling of small-caps and hard-to-borrow stocks, or charge locate fees per position. TradeZero's inventory is deeper for the small-cap short niche than most retail brokers.

For active short-side traders — especially those trading small-cap runners, catalyst-driven shorts, or the "pattern day trader" style of aggressive short-selling — TradeZero is often the specialist choice. Robinhood, Fidelity, and Schwab don't offer the same short-locate depth.
