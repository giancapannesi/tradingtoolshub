What RebelsFunding Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: no time limit on evaluation and the $640K scaling ceiling. Most prop firms require you to hit profit targets within 30-60 days — that time pressure forces suboptimal trades and drives failure. RebelsFunding removes it entirely: pass whenever you're ready. For patient swing traders and those with irregular market availability (part-time traders), that flexibility is a real edge over FTMO or Apex.

$640K funded ceiling is higher than most competitors' base tiers. Combined with the no-time-limit rule, RebelsFunding lets consistent traders scale to institutional-sized funded capital without evaluation-cycle pressure — a materially different value proposition from pass-fast-or-die competitors.
