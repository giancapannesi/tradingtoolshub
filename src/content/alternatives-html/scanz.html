Why Traders Consider Scanz Alternatives

Scanz is a professional real-time stock scanner with 100+ filters, best-in-class news streaming, and Level 2 quotes — built for active day traders scanning for momentum and news catalysts. Three drivers push traders elsewhere: $99+/mo Pro tier, desktop-focused (no mobile counterpart), and data feed costs stack on top of subscription.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want AI-driven scanning: Trade Ideas. If you want free US screener: Finviz. If you want multi-asset + Pine Script: TradingView. If you want plain-English scans: TC2000. If you want news + squawk: Benzinga Pro. If you want options flow: Unusual Whales.

What Scanz Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: the real-time news integration and Level 2 + scanner fusion. Scanz aggregates news from multiple sources into the scanner UI — you can see a scan hit and immediately read the news catalyst driving it, without switching to a separate news platform. That workflow speed matters for gap-and-go and momentum trades where seconds count.

Level 2 quotes embedded in the scanner mean order-book context sits next to the momentum signal. Most scanners (Finviz, TC2000) require you to open a separate Level 2 window. Scanz consolidates the momentum trader's key inputs into one workspace.
