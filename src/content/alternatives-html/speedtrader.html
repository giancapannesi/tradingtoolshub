Why Traders Look Beyond SpeedTrader

SpeedTrader is a direct-market-access broker for active day traders and scalpers with sub-millisecond execution, multiple routing options, and per-share pricing. Genuine institutional-adjacent positioning. Three limits push traders elsewhere: high account minimums (typically $25K+ for pro platform), steep learning curve for direct-access tools, and margin rates higher than Interactive Brokers.

The alternative depends on why you'd leave. If you want lowest margin rates + institutional pricing: Interactive Brokers dominates. If you want pro-grade execution at lower minimum: Cobra Trading. If you want direct-access with routing options at similar tier: Lightspeed. If you want modern all-in-one: TradeStation.

What SpeedTrader Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: direct-market-access routing with sub-millisecond execution and per-share pricing that scales with volume. For high-volume day traders who need routing control (avoid PFOF, choose your ECN, minimize slippage), SpeedTrader's execution quality is materially different from PFOF-based commission-free brokers.

The trader-facing platform is built for speed — hotkeys, one-click execution, Level 2 depth of book. For scalpers whose edge is speed, that's a real feature vs slower web platforms.
