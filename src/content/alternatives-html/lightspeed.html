Where Lightspeed Isn't the Best Fit

Lightspeed is a direct-access broker for active US day traders with ultra-low latency execution (100+ routing destinations), aggressive per-share volume discounts (down to $0.001 for high volume), and the purpose-built Lightspeed Trader Pro platform with hotkeys and Level 2. Three ceilings push traders elsewhere: $25,000 minimum for desktop platform, $10,000 for web (excludes smaller accounts), $130/month platform fee unless offset by commission volume, and margin rates 8.50%-11.00% significantly higher than Interactive Brokers.

The alternative depends on why you'd leave. If you need lower minimums / no platform fee: Interactive Brokers (Lite) or Cobra Trading. If you need tighter margin rates: Interactive Brokers dominates. If you need options + stocks together with pro tools: thinkorswim (Schwab). If you need simpler UX without pro-broker complexity: Fidelity Active Trader Pro or Schwab.

What Lightspeed Does Better Than Alternatives

Two genuine differentiators: direct-market-access routing to 100+ destinations and per-share pricing that drops to $0.001 at volume. For high-volume day traders (10K+ shares/day), Lightspeed's cost structure beats any per-trade broker. Interactive Brokers is competitive but Lightspeed's routing options are broader.

The Lightspeed Trader Pro platform is genuinely built for speed — hotkeys, one-click order entry, Level 2 depth of book. Compared to a broker web platform, the execution speed difference matters during volatile opens/closes and news events.

The Bottom Line

Lightspeed is the direct-access pro-broker for high-volume US day traders who trade 10K+ shares daily and need routing control. For everyone else, the platform fee + high minimum + high margin rates make it uneconomical. Interactive Brokers matches Lightspeed on pricing at scale without the fixed platform fee, plus offers global multi-asset. For chart-driven traders, TradeStation.
