When OptionStrat Isn't Enough

OptionStrat is a visual options strategy builder + P&L calculator + real-time flow scanner. Its visual P&L diagrams are best-in-class — nothing else in retail comes close. Free tier is genuinely usable (not a teaser), and paid tiers stay affordable ($9-29/mo). Three limits push traders elsewhere: no broker integration (can't execute directly from strategies), no backtesting against historical data, and live data restricted to non-professional users due to exchange licensing.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you need execution built in: thinkorswim, tastytrade, or Interactive Brokers with a strategy tab. If you need backtesting: OptionAlpha or a broker's platform. If you need deeper flow scanning: Unusual Whales, InsiderFinance, or BlackBoxStocks. If you're a professional trader: institutional-grade platforms without the retail-tier data limits.

What OptionStrat Does Better Than Anyone

The visual P&L diagrams and Options Optimizer that scans thousands of strategy combinations for the best structure are genuinely best-in-class in retail options tools. No competitor's strategy visualization is as clean or intuitive. For options traders who think in terms of payoff diagrams, OptionStrat is the reference tool.

The free tier is usable — not a teaser. You can model spreads, see P&L, view flow, and make real decisions without paying. That's rare in the options-flow space where every peer requires payment upfront.
