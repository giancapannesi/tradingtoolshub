Why Someone Considers ChartMill Alternatives

ChartMill is a Belgium-based stock screener with 180+ filters, proprietary trend and value indicators, and heat-map visualization. Strong for European equities and technical-fundamental hybrid screens. Three drivers push traders elsewhere: US market data isn't as deep as US-native tools, UI is functional but dated, and free tier is limited compared to Finviz.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want free US stock screener: Finviz. If you want multi-asset scanning: TradingView. If you want deep fundamentals: Stock Rover. If you want AI-driven scanning: Trade Ideas. If you want plain-English screens: TC2000.

What ChartMill Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: European equities coverage and the proprietary Trend/Value/Growth composite scores. Most US-focused screeners have shallow international coverage. ChartMill covers Euronext, Deutsche Börse, LSE and other European exchanges well — genuinely useful for European traders or US traders looking at ADRs and international diversification. The composite scores compress multiple technical/fundamental signals into single 0-10 ratings, which is a nice "at-a-glance" filter that most competitors don't offer.

Backtesting on saved screens is decent. You can define a screen, backtest it against historical data, and see how a mechanical strategy would have performed — a feature Finviz free lacks entirely.
