Why Traders Consider OneFunded Alternatives

OneFunded is a London-based prop firm offering evaluation challenges from $23 with up to 95% profit split. Extremely low entry fee makes it accessible for testing prop trading without significant capital risk. What OneFunded Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: the $23 entry fee and the 95% profit split ceiling. Most prop firms charge $155+ for the smallest challenge — a real barrier for traders wanting to test whether they can pass evaluation mechanics before committing capital. OneFunded's low entry lets you try prop trading for the cost of lunch, which is genuinely differentiated in the market.

95% profit split at higher tiers is meaningful. FTMO caps at 80% standard; FundedNext goes to 90%. OneFunded's 95% ceiling means an extra 5-15% of your funded account's profits stays with you. On a $100K funded account making 10%/month, that's $500-$1,500 extra per month.
