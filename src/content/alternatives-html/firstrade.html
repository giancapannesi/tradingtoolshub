Why Someone Looks Beyond Firstrade

Firstrade is a commission-free US broker with $0 stock, ETF, and options trades — no per-contract fees on options, which is unusual. Cost-conscious traders find real value here. Three limits push traders elsewhere: basic charting and research tools compared to Fidelity or Schwab, no futures, forex, or crypto, and fewer educational resources than established full-service brokers.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want serious tools + $0 commissions: Fidelity or Schwab (thinkorswim). If you want cheapest options with deep education: tastytrade + tastylive. If you want multi-asset access: Interactive Brokers. If you want social/community: Public.com or Robinhood.

What Firstrade Does Better Than Some Peers

Two things: $0 per-contract options commissions (rare in the US market) and the low-cost broker positioning without hidden fees. Robinhood also offers $0 per-contract options but has PFOF concerns; tastytrade charges $1 to open. Firstrade genuinely delivers zero option commissions.

For a cost-conscious retail investor who trades stocks + ETFs + some options and doesn't need advanced charts, Firstrade delivers a functional platform at the lowest total cost — no platform fees, no minimums, no per-contract options fees.
