Why Traders Consider TC2000 Scanner Alternatives

TC2000 by Worden Brothers is a fast, US-focused stock scanner and charting platform voted #1 for stock analysis by Investor's Business Daily. EasyScan lets non-technical traders build sophisticated scans in plain English. Three drivers push traders elsewhere: US equities focus (limited international coverage), desktop-centric (mobile is stripped-down), and integrated brokerage is US-only.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want AI-driven scanning: Trade Ideas. If you want free US screener: Finviz. If you want multi-asset + Pine Script: TradingView. If you want professional real-time news + Level 2: Scanz. If you want free with broker + ThinkScript: thinkorswim.

What TC2000 Scanner Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: the EasyScan plain-English builder and the tight scanner→chart→broker loop. Most professional scanners require writing custom logic in a proprietary scripting language (ThinkScript, Pine Script, TC's own EasyLanguage variants). EasyScan lets you build sophisticated scans with drop-downs and English phrases — "stocks that gapped up 3% with volume above 200%" is a few clicks, not a script. That accessibility is real.

The chart engine is fast. TC2000 renders charts and updates data faster than most competitors, and the integrated brokerage (TC2000 Brokerage) means scanner hits become one-click orders. Trade Ideas is more powerful; TC2000 is more accessible.
