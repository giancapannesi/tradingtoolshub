Why Traders Look Beyond CenterPoint Securities

CenterPoint Securities is a premium DMA broker for professional day traders and short sellers, known for the industry's deepest short-locate inventory and personal risk-desk service. Three drivers push traders elsewhere: high minimum funding ($30K+), tiered commission complexity, and focus on high-volume professional traders — casual retail users won't get value.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want similar short-locate DMA: Cobra Trading, Lightspeed, or SpeedTrader. If you want lowest global commissions: Interactive Brokers. If you want best options + charting free: thinkorswim. If you want zero-commission simplicity: Webull. If you want options + tastylive: tastytrade.

What CenterPoint Securities Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: the short-locate inventory depth and the risk-desk relationships. CenterPoint is one of the few brokers that actively sources hard-to-borrow shorts on low-float momentum names — many other brokers can't source at any price. For traders who short small-cap runners, CenterPoint's locate desk is a genuine edge that free brokers can't replicate.

Personal risk-desk relationships mean margin calls, position sizing, and edge-case questions get human attention. Interactive Brokers charges lower commissions but treats every trader as a number. CenterPoint's institutional-style service justifies the higher barriers for professional-tier traders.
