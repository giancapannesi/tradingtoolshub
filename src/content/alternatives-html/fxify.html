What FXIFY Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: broker-backing for execution stability and 24-hour payouts. Many prop firms route trades to internal simulated environments that behave differently from live markets. Broker-backed firms like FXIFY hedge to real execution, which reduces the "sim-vs-live" divergence. 24-hour payout processing is genuinely fast — most competitors take 3-5 business days.

300+ instruments with EA support means traders who already use automated strategies from MT4/MT5 don't need to rebuild. That portability is important for algo traders considering prop-firm scaling.
