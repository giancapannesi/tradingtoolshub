Where Trade Ideas Doesn't Fit

Trade Ideas is an AI-powered stock scanner known for the Holly AI system that runs preset strategies and surfaces trade setups automatically. It's the reference AI-scanner in retail — Holly's decision engine is genuinely different from rule-based screeners. Three limits push traders elsewhere: expensive ($107-178/mo), US stocks only, and steep learning curve to interpret Holly's alerts profitably.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want a free/cheap scanner: Finviz. If plain-English scanning is your priority: TC2000's EasyScan. If you want AI-driven charting instead of pure scanning: TrendSpider or Kavout. If multi-asset scanning: TradingView's screeners.

What Trade Ideas Does Better Than Alternatives

The Holly AI system is Trade Ideas' unique product. No other retail scanner has an AI that generates trade signals from preset strategies without user configuration. For traders who want to outsource strategy discovery to an AI rather than build scans manually, Holly is genuinely differentiated.

The real-time execution speed and scan depth are competitive with institutional-tier scanners at a lower price point than institutional data feeds. For active day traders who need fast surfacing of setups during market hours, Trade Ideas is priced correctly for what it delivers.
