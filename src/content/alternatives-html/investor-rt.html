Why Someone Looks Beyond Investor/RT

Investor/RT by Linn Software is a professional charting platform with VolumeScope footprint charting and OrderFlowMetrics — serious pro-level tools with decades of order-flow depth. Its niche is order-flow specialists who want fine-grained control. Three limits push traders elsewhere: expensive and steep learning curve, Windows-only, and smaller community + fewer educational resources than Sierra Chart or Bookmap.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want the industry-standard depth heatmap: Bookmap. If you want modern order-flow with active community: ATAS. If you want deep customization + backtesting: Sierra Chart. If you want focused footprint at lower cost: Exocharts.

What Investor/RT Does Better Than Alternatives

Two things: VolumeScope footprint charting and OrderFlowMetrics. Both are proprietary implementations that some pro traders prefer over Bookmap or Sierra Chart's approaches. For traders with a specific workflow built around Linn Software's tools, switching means learning a new metric system.

Decades of continuous development means Investor/RT has quirks + power features that newer platforms don't. For a small pro user base, that's an advantage; for new users, it's a barrier.

Cost + Feature Comparison

  • Investor/RT: Higher pricing tier + VolumeScope + OrderFlowMetrics. Windows-only.
  • Bookmap: Global Plus $118/mo. Standard/Digital cheaper.
  • ATAS: ~$59-99/mo. Active community + education.
  • Sierra Chart: $26-164/mo + exchange data fees.
  • Exocharts: ~$29/mo. Focused, cross-platform.
  • NinjaTrader: Free with delayed data. Lease $99/mo or lifetime $1,499.
  • Interactive Brokers: Low commissions + global multi-asset.

The Bottom Line

Investor/RT is the pro-tier proprietary order-flow platform for traders committed to Linn Software's workflow. If you're not already deep in that ecosystem, Bookmap is the industry standard.
