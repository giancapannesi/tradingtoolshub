When MarketSmith / MarketSurge Doesn't Fit

MarketSmith (now branded as MarketSurge in IBD's current product family) is Investor's Business Daily's growth-stock research platform built around CAN SLIM ratings, chart pattern recognition, and IBD's proprietary Composite Rating. It's the reference tool for growth investors who follow William O'Neil's methodology. Three limits push traders elsewhere: expensive at $149.95/mo, CAN SLIM-specific methodology that doesn't fit value or momentum styles, and US stocks only.

The alternative depends on your style. If you want free CAN SLIM-adjacent screening: Finviz's technical + fundamental filters can approximate the setup. If you want modern chart analysis + AI: TrendSpider. If you want fundamental screener: Stock Rover. If you want comprehensive research terminal: Koyfin Pro.

What MarketSmith / MarketSurge Does Better

Two things: proprietary IBD ratings (Composite, EPS, RS, SMR) and CAN SLIM-native workflows. No competitor calculates the exact same rating structure, and no competitor is as focused on growth-investor methodology. If you follow CAN SLIM strictly, you're paying for a purpose-built tool.

Weekly Big Picture columns, MarketSurge's improved chart pattern detection, and the IBD 50 / IBD Big Cap 20 lists are proprietary content you can't get elsewhere. For growth-style traders who value the IBD ecosystem, staying makes sense.

The Bottom Line

MarketSmith / MarketSurge is the CAN SLIM growth-investor platform. If you follow IBD's methodology closely, the proprietary ratings + IBD content aren't fully replaceable elsewhere. If you want a cheaper toolkit that approximates the setup, combine Finviz (screening) + TrendSpider (chart patterns) + Stock Rover (fundamentals) — total cost still under MarketSmith.
