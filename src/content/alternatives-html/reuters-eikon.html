Why Traders Consider Reuters Eikon Alternatives (Eikon Retired June 2025)

Reuters Eikon was retired by LSEG on June 30, 2025 and replaced by LSEG Workspace. Existing Eikon users must migrate to Workspace or an alternative. Three drivers push traders elsewhere during the migration: Workspace UI changes require re-training, pricing tiers may shift vs. legacy Eikon contracts, and this is a natural moment to reassess whether Bloomberg or a lower-cost alternative better fits current needs.

The alternative depends on the gap. If you want the direct successor: LSEG Workspace. If you want the institutional gold standard: Bloomberg Terminal. If you want terminal UI at 1/50th price: Koyfin Pro. If you want developer market-data API: Polygon.io. If you want long-form research: Seeking Alpha.

What Eikon Did Better Than Most Alternatives

Two things (historical): the Reuters news division and the fixed-income depth. Reuters News (now part of LSEG) has one of the largest global newsroom footprints in finance — 2,600+ journalists across 200 locations delivering exclusive stories, breaking news, and market color. That newsroom is what Eikon subscribers were really paying for, alongside terminal-grade data. Workspace inherits that news pipeline.

Fixed-income coverage was one of Eikon's genuine institutional strengths. Bond pricing, yield curves, credit analytics — Eikon's fixed-income tools were competitive with Bloomberg for many institutional workflows at lower cost. Workspace continues this focus.
